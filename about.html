ABOUT
INTRODUCING THE MODERN WORLD MAGAZINE -Your fastest source for news and the modern way of fighting information and media illiteracy.

The mission of this Magazine is to fight information illiteracy to everyone, the fortunate or less marginalised people. This is to ensure that information is not gate kept and that it's available to eveyone through news and articles that will feed us information that we have never seen before. We are here for the people and to ensure that we feed them with information that will make them better people tomorrow.

We are the future of young people of the modern world, we feed modern people with the latest news and information.

Modern World Magazine is an idependent, student-run publication with a mission to deliver untold stories and perspectives often overlooked by mainstream media. The magazine is commited uncovering and and sharing vital information that has never been delivered by any other organisation, while also providing our communities with rich,authentic and valuable content. We aim top empower, inform and inspire through fearless journalism and meaningful storytelling.
