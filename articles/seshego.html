Two people were found dead at zone 6, Seshego

Published on June 18, 2025

Man and woman whom their age range from 34 to 40, were found dead where they had gun wounds on their bodies. Their bodies were found on a pool of blood next to overnight accommodation. Investigations are still ongoing.
